You know, now that this has been brought up, I might as well put down some of my own thoughts on a hypothetical alternate 'Fallen Heiress' scenario where Ruby and Opal were in a different family. How about instead of having the whole family hate Ruby, what if different families have different challenges for Ruby instead of '_the whole family hates Ruby and doesn't want her racing_'? For example…  
  
**HYPOTHETICAL SYMBOLI CLAN EXAMPLE:**  
  

Spoiler: Symboli Clan Fallen Heiress Quest Ideas

In a hypothetical 'Symboli Ruby' quest, Ruby might be the caught up in a 'power struggle' between Rudolf, who seemingly doesn't want Ruby in the academy because of what Opal did, and Sirius, who went out of her way to pull favors in the URA to get Ruby in despite Ruby having (supposedly) failing the entrance exam (which was sabotaged of course).  
  
Instead of Ruby's time in Tracen being characterized as just her being the cheater's daughter that is hounded by a mega-corp, Ruby instead becomes the focal point between Sirius 'problem children' and Rudolf's and the Trancen staff's authority whether she likes it or not. This draws a line right down the center of Tracen between people who support Rudolf (which include the Staff and fans of Rudolf like Teio), and people who support Sirius.  
  
The conflicts further complicated by the fact Rudolf (who isn't actually secretly evil), despite being the strongest opponent to Ruby's continued enrolment at Tracen, does not actually hate Ruby at all. Quite the opposite in fact – it's all an act she puts up because Rudolf is terrified for Ruby's life: in this scenario, she knows Opal' enemies, who are supernatural in nature and beyond Rudolf' ability to counter, set her aunt up and destroyed her life and career. Rudolf now fears her cousin could be the next target, and want Ruby out for her own safety. Sirius unaware of this, is furious Rudolf is being a bitch to their cousin – she can understand the old family heads and the URA being antagonistic, but she knows Rudolf and Rudolf's 'animosity' against Ruby is a mystery that Sirius and Ruby want to solve.  
  
The dilemma for Ruby and the players would be helping Sirius, and thus helping her own self-interest in her sports career and the lives of disadvantaged racers, reduces Rudolf's control over the academy. This would harm Rudolf's already limited ability to keep a lid on the supernatural stuff, making her magical girl life harder and the harm caused by the bad guys worse for everyone involved.  
  
Thus, this hypothetical Symboli Ruby quest would not be 'Ruby the underdog v. stuck up family', but 'Ruby the underdog caught between the loyalist faction of the family that dislikes her, and the smaller rebel faction that supports her'. This could introduce a faction mechanic in the game in faction to the SLs where Ruby's actions could help Rudolf and/or Sirius' influence over the academy wax and/or wane as she wins races and helps certain people.

  
**HYPOTHETICAL GOLD CLAN IDEA:**  
  

Spoiler: Gold Clan Fallen Heiress Quest Ideas

As for a Gold clan story, instead of the clan suddenly turning into malicious versions of themselves, we could really play into the 'chaotic sort-of-good, crime family' image some versions of them have going and extrapolate a scenario from there. While Opal did cheat in this situation and thus was exiled, many members of the family didn't hold it against Ruby and instead want her to be the redemption of the family name. Unfortunately the Gold family, being the Gold family, expresses this in the worst/strangest possible way they could.  
  
Taking a page from the Gold Clan Amnesiac backstory idea, Ruby in this case had NOT intended to enroll in Tracen. In fact she was planning on exploring other possible careers for uma musume such as being a package courier, fitness trainer, uma idol… as suggested this version of Opal did something truly unforgivable (as expected of a Gold to take things too far) and Ruby wants to distance herself from that life as much as possible, even if she gives up on her own childhood dreams to do so.  
  
Then one day, while she is minding her own business readying herself for her future career, she grabbed off the street, stuffed in a sack, hauled to Tracen in a black van, brought before Stay Gold, Orfevre and Dream Journey in a pitch-black room with a single light and chair…. and told in no uncertain terms her mother owes a debt to the family, and Ruby now has to pay it.  
  
What they meant: _"Okay, Rubes. Your mom fucked up, but we think you got potential. The stain on the family honor needs to go away, and we think if you win a few races legit, all will be good. Besides, we kind of miss you and want you around. Sound good, baby girl?"_  
  
What Ruby heard: _"We're the Yakuza. Your mom fucked us well and proper, and then ran off like a coward. The stain on the family's honor is a debt that needs to go away your bloodline owes us. You have to pay it. If you don't, well… some of us don't miss you. Sound good?"_  
  
Orfevre… probably shouldn't have done the talking.  
  
This hypothetical scenario would then involve Ruby, who had been wanting to do something else and distance herself from her mother (who she genuinely hates here), trying desperately to win a G1 because she thinks Stay Gold will give her concrete boots at the bottom of Tokyo Bay if she does not. Meanwhile the Gold Clan, thinking this is their chance to welcome their missing daughter home and that they want to train her to a G1 level, try to help in the only way they know how (i.e. over the top antics). They also somehow give everyone else (the staff, the students, the media, all of society, etc…) the impression the entirely of the Gold Clan hates that Ruby is in the Academy – socially isolating Ruby since most other students are now afraid of her and giving a new challenge to the players to overcome the Clan's influence and secure much needed SLs (outside the Gold Clan).  
  
The fact the Gold Clan doesn't actually hate Ruby as a person will obviously only be known to the GM in this hypothetical quest, and the hilarity would come from progressing the SLs of the Gold Clan members to a point to discover 'wait, Golshi doesn't want to kill me if I don't win a G1' (instantly flipping the -1 SL from Ruby's POV to the +4 it actually is from Goldshi's POV)? There might even be some kind of sanity/panic gauge ala games like Delta Green, Mothership or the Aliens TTRPG, where this version of Ruby, who thinks her family are Yazuka, believes death by Stay Gold or Gold Ship is a very real possibility, can pick up conditions/phobias players would have to manage in addition to her mood. A challenging problem, depending on what kind of evil monster she has to fight as a magical girl.  
  
This also is very different from the Satono Heiress Quest in that the members of the Gold Clan, unaware Ruby thinks they are Yakuza out for her, actively seek Ruby out instead of avoiding her. Whereas Diamond and Crown are distant threats and long term goals us players are planning towards, in this hypothetical quest the players would have an opposite problem – each turn, roll d6 to see which member of the Gold Clan decides to pop in and say hello. No such thing as avoiding them, Ruby my dear… THEY WILL FIND YOU.

  
…anyway, those are some of my random thoughts. My brain goes to some really random places sometimes.
