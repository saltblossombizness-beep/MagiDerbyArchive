The Platinum Generation. Our competition - and, hopefully, our friends and rivals. These umas are all going to be very relevant to Ruby's career and social life in the future, so it behooves us to give them all an in-depth examination.  
  
**Durendal:** _"My holy sword shines with swift brilliance, It cuts down all who would oppose you! I am Durandal, a knight of oath and loyalty!"_  
-Style: End Closer  
-Distance: Sprint/Mile  
-Description: Not to be confused with Duramente. Durendal roleplays as a knight out of legend, but in truth loves to be praised. She's usually paired with Calstone Light O, a solid contender for the single funniest uma in the franchise and who I desperately hope appears in the quest, who is so strange Durendal is the 'straight man' in their dynamic. It's hard to say how she'll behave, plucked from her 'roster' of friends and rivals.  
  
**Kawakami Princess:** _"Oh-ho-ho! I am Kawakami Princess...yes, a princess above all princesses! I'll daintily knock you off your feet at the races!"_  
-Style: Late Surger  
-Distance: Mile/Medium  
-Description: A rambunctious tomboy with an odd idea of what it means to be a princess. Inspired by what is strongly implied to be Pretty Cure, she's intent on using her royal strength to flatten any bad guys and competitors who stand against her. Being mentored by King Halo.  
  
**Meisho Taburu:** _"Hm."_  
-Style: Front Runner  
-Distance: (Currently Unknown, at least Medium)  
-Description: Ruby's roommate and a quest OC. There's still a lot to be learned about Taburu, but despite her stoic, quiet demeanor, she's still a member of the Gold Clan - thus, not to be underestimated. She makes friends out of bullies by demolishing them on the track.  
  
**Happy Meek:** _A calm Umamusume. Her good fortune blossoms late!_  
-Style: Mediocre at All  
-Distance: Mediocre at All  
-Description: Happy Meek is calm, unassuming, even outright placid uma. Trained by Aoi Kiryuin, she's got a remarkably versatile skillset - being able to run literally all distances and styles, but this is also her weakness; she doesn't truly _specialize_ in anything either. In the game, Kiryuin struggles to train her with conventional strategies - it remains to be seen how well she can manage in this quest.  
  
**Mayano Top Gun:** _"When inspiration strikes, Maya takes flight! Copy that! ☆"_  
-Style: All(!)  
-Distance: Medium/Long  
-Description: The youthful genius, having leapfrogged grades through her raw talent, but now struggling to hack it in a truly competitive environment without having learned the hard, boring habits of studying and training. It'll take time for Mayano to truly lock in as a racer, but in the meantime she's still a fearsome foe with an intuitive grasp on the flow of the track.  
  
**Aston Machan:** _"I'm Machan. Aston Machan. One day I'll become the world's mascot. Please keep your eyes right on me."_  
-Style: Front Runner/Pace Chaser  
-Distance: Sprint/Mile  
-Description: Machan is one of the more explicitly supernatural umas in the roster, like Manhattan Cafe and Neo Universe. Her IRL horse counterpart had a short and tough career, dying before she could become a true rival to Vodka and Daiwa Scarlet, and her career is one of the most explicit about the nature of reincarnation and how hard it is to defy fate. It's a suckers bet to say there'll be something odd going on with her in this quest, and out of all the umas listed here, I'd trust her 'official' stats the least. Aston is intent on getting screentime no matter what it takes.  
  
**Cheval Grand:** _"...Cheval Grand... You know... I know this may seem like a bit of a stretch, but... Someday, I'll do my best to be great... No, I will be great."_  
-Style: Pace Chaser/Late Surger  
-Distance: Medium/Long  
-Description: The socially awkward middle child of the V trio. Compared to her vivacious, glamorous, and cheerful siblings, Cheval thinks herself bland and mediocre. She fixates on Kitasan Black as an example of everything she wants to be, regarding her with a mix of envy and respect. Despite her deep struggles with self-loathing and confidence, Cheval has serious potential as a Stayer that just needs to be tapped.  
  
**Vivlos:** _"I'm Vivlos! Some day, I'm going to race in the glitzy city of Dubai, and become a celeb~! Let's go together! I've already made up my mind~!"_  
-Style: Late Surger  
-Distance: Mile/Medium  
-Description: Vivlos is the very image of the spoiled youngest sibling, and her dream of winning the Dubai race seems superficial and vapid. But the girl is more self-aware than even her sisters give her credit for. She loves the finer things in life, but she also wants to be taken seriously and make her family proud. As a racer, she's got good instincts and an excellent blitz, but struggles to put on weight and build stamina - the opposite problems of "Chevvachi~."  
  
**Mihono Bourbon:** _"Target acquired. Commencing Operation: Triple Crown."_  
-Style: Front Runner  
-Distance: Mile/Medium/Long (Mediocre at Sprints)  
-Description: Rumored to be more machine than uma, Satono Ruby isn't the only member of the Platinum Generation undergoing a grueling training regimen to push for the Medium and Long distances. Naturally a sprinter, Bourbon has practically rebuilt herself in pursuit of her dream - the Classics Triple Crown, defying all common wisdom in doing so. Rivals Kitasan Black and Mayano Top Gun for the single strongest racer present.  
  
**Rice Shower:** _"Rice only brings misfortune to everyone... I really... really hope I can change that!"_  
-Style: Pace Chaser/Front Runner (Mediocre at Late Surger)  
-Distance: Medium/Long  
-Description: An uma who would normally be considered the biggest woobie of the generation, if it weren't for poor Ruby stealing her spot. At the beginning, Rice is seriously struggling to perform, with her shyness and lack of confidence causing her to outright no-show scrimmage races. Nevertheless, there are the bones of a seriously talented stayer in the quivering girl. So much of Rice Shower's character and reputation is built into shattering the dreams of Bourbon and McQueen, so it's hard to say how this version of her will turn out.  
  
**Satono Diamond:** _"My name is Satono Diamond. There is no such thing as a predetermined fate. I'll shine a light on the future with my own legs!"_  
-Style: Late Surger/Pace Chaser  
-Distance: Medium/Long  
-Description: Ruby's estranged sister. I wrote up a very detailed analysis of her, now stored in the information tab. Expect her to hang back most the race and burst for the finish at just the right moment, relying on her track sense and planning to pull it off.  
  
**Kitasan Black:** _"I'm Kitasan Black! Aiming for a race that's as exciting as a festival where everyone is having fun. I'll run with all my strength!"_  
-Style: Front Runner/Pace Chaser (Mediocre at Late Surger)  
-Distance: Medium/Long (Mediocre at Mile)  
-Description: What can be said about this successor to the Black Beast, that hasn't already? Ruby has one hell of a rival waiting for her on the track.  
  
  
Templar's Notes:  
  
1. The game and quest are fundamentally different formats. This is rather obvious, but it does have serious implications for racing strategy. For example, in the game, specialization is infinitely more valuable than versatility due to its inherent nature. The quest format allows for much greater granularity, however. Individual races are a much bigger deal, and planning around your opposition is an important part of it. In that sense, having versatility becomes a much more serious advantage, due to having access to a larger toolkit to adjust strategy for specific circumstances, foes, and so forth. With that in mind, Mayano Top Gun and Happy Meek become more serious foes than you'd otherwise think.  
  
2. The majority of the 'Platinum Generation' is geared for Medium and Long races. This is where we should expect the majority of the action and competition to take place; the Kikkua Sho in particular is going to be incredibly intense due to four separate winners of that leg of the Triple Crown being present (Rice Shower, Kitasan Black, Satono Diamond, Mayano Top Gun). Fortunately, Durandal and Aston Machan can still run Miles, otherwise they'd be all but totally left out.  
  
3. There are three mares present in the Generation: Aston Machan, Vivlos, and Kawakami Princess. They'll be running the Triple Tiara on their lonesome, it seems, unless Ruby decides to switch her earing placement.
