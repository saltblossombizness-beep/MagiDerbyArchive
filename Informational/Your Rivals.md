I'd love to try and challenge the triple crown, but the lineup this year is so insanely stacked I can scarcely imagine it being possible.  
  
We have multiple double double crown winners in this gen, each of which specialise in a different distance. Mihono Bourbon excels at shorter distances, as does Meisho Tabaru and Kawakami Princess, Kitasan Black should have a death-grip over middle distances while doing well in longs, while Rice Shower is fully focused on dominating long distances. Satono Diamond is the unexpected wildcard for the Triple Crown as well.  
  
Basically, for every distance that exists in the Triple Crown, we have _multiple_ runners that specialise at that distance. The thought that we can compete in their specialities and beat every single one of them is honestly a complete fantasy.  
  
I'm not denying that we _could_ do this, only that it depends entirely on if the QM will let us. Because the opposition they have set us against is so _**ridiculously**_ stacked that we might only have the faintest sliver of a chance _**if**_ Salt lets us build Ruby into a death machine of a racer.  
  
To make it clearer how absurdly stacked our generation is:  
  
Threats for the Satsuki Sho: Meisho Tabaru, Mihono Bourbon, Satono Diamond, Kitasan Black,  
Threats for the Japan Derby: Mihono Bourbon, Mayano Top Gun, Satono Diamond, Kitasan Black, Rice Shower  
Threats for the Kikuka Sho: Mihono Bourbon, Happy Meek, Mayano Top Gun, Cherval Grand, Satono Diamond, Kitasan Black, Rice Shower

**Meisho Tabaru**  
Career is still ongoing, but I can work with the stats we have. [Racing Record](https://www.jbis.jp/horse/0001335651/record/).  
  
Tabaru is a great escapist, and looking at the race records you can see that standing out very strongly. They either win, place in the top 5 or completely bomb in last place.  
  
Of their graded races, they've won a G3 at 1800m, a G2 at 2200m and a G1 at 2200m.  
  
But, they also challenged the Satsuki Sho, the first leg of the Triple Crown at 2000m and absolutely bombed the race, finishing in 17th place. They also challenged the Kikuka Sho, the final leg at 3000m meters and came in 16th. The Arima Kinen at 2500m wasn't much better, at 13th. Notably they also challenged the Tenno Sho Autumn at 2000m and came 6th, and the Dubai Turf at 1800m and came 5th.  
  
Generally, looking at their race records, they tend to perform well between 1800-2200m. but anything further than 2200m is completely out of their striking range. They do well in miles, which means if she enters, she'll be a fierce competitor in the Asahi Hai. However escape runners have an absolutely fatal flaw. if you can disrupt their escape before it starts, then they will most likely just completely collapse and perform terribly.  
  
If she's in one of our races and it's within her range, I heavily suggest disrupting her escape at the gate. If it's a longer race such as 2000m or above, then our best move is to ignore her and run our own race as a frontrunner, then burst out at the end and overtake her as she starts slowing down. She is not a threat at 2400m and above.  
  
We also have the spicy option of leveraging our excellent stamina and, if it's a mile race, _directly competing with her_ as a Great Escapist. With our stamina we should be able to endure the death-ride for longer than her and forcibly claim first place, which will probably completely ruin her race.  
  
  
**Mihono Bourbon**  
Mihono had an absolutely terrifying reign of domination upon the tracks. 7 wins in 8 starts, and 1 second place finish. What an absolute monster. They also set _two_ track records. [Racing Record](https://www.jbis.jp/horse/0000220336/record/).  
  
They took first in a race at 1000m, two firsts at 1600m (one of which being the G1 Asahi Hai, so we will definitely see her there), a first at the G2 Fuji TV Sho at 1800m, then winning the Satsuki Sho 2000m, Japan Derby 2400m, Kyoto Shimbun Hai 2200m before finally having their reign of terror stopped by Rice Shower at the Kikuka Sho 3000m.  
  
They are absolutely a top-tier competitor at every distance, nothing is out of their range.  
  
She's noted to have only ever run as a frontrunner, and likely has absolutely zero experience running in any other style. So we can know for certain that she _will_ be running as a Frontrunner, ironclad certainty there.  
  
She is the opponent we must be focusing on the most for our ambition to win the Asahi Hai, because she'll come to challenge us for this race, there is no doubt about it.  
  
  
**Kawakami Princess**  
Kawakami Princess is one of the lesser threats on the board, but she seems to be an early bloomer. In that she grows rapidly stronger earlier than everyone else, then tapers off later. [Racing Record](https://www.jbis.jp/horse/0000759762/record/).  
  
She opened her career with straight wins from 1400m (x2) then 1800m, Japanese Oaks 2400m and finally the Shuka Sho 2000m. She claimed the first two legs of the Triple Tiara quite convincingly, but at this point her racing record falls off of a cliff. She never won another race again, only coming 2nd twice and 3rd twice. Notably, one of those 2nd's was the Queen Elizabeth II Cup at 2200m, which is very respectable, but still not first place.  
  
Overall, I do not believe she will be a notable challenger for us. She never took the Asahi Hai irl, and she's aiming for the Triple Tiara, which means she won't be competing with us for the Triple Crown.  
  
If we do run across her, she's a very powerful late surger, but it seems that her performance is very much front loaded into her career. She should perform well at Miles and Middle distance. Key suggestion for deafeating her is to disrupt her final kick with mind-games or a well-timed frenzy. Trying to lead her to getting boxed in should work well too.  
  
  
**Happy Meek**  
Happy Meek is a fake Uma, so there is no real life race record to analyse. But what we do know about her is that she's even more of a freak than Mayano. She has a good affinity for every distance, racing style _and_ she can run of turf and dirt.  
We can expect her to show up literally anywhere, running in any style. It's almost impossible for us to predict, and she might be like us, capable of switching racing style mid race as events suit her.  
  
_Dangerously_ adaptable, but we also know that her Trainer is somewhat floundering with her because she can't figure out what to do with such a perfectly rounded racer. We'll likely compete with her often, but the early parts of her career will likely be much weaker than her late career, when her Trainer figures out what the hell to do with her.  
  
She may spook us at the Asahi Hai, but once again it's so early in her career that her training issues might not be sorted out yet.  
  
We have no clue if she'll compete in the Triple Crown or Triple Tiara, but either way she shouldn't be too much of a danger for the first two legs of the Triple Crown.  
  
  
**Mayano Top Gun**  
Mayano has a very weird career record. She's quite threatening, but it seems that a lot of that threat is back-loaded in longer races. [Racing Record](https://www.jbis.jp/horse/0000255813/record/).  
  
Mayano started her career very poorly, running 9 non-graded races and only winning three of them, though she came 3rd in five. Notably, all of these races were below 2200m. She then went on to take 2nd in a G2 at 2000m and another at 2200m. After this she took back to back 1st's in the Kikuka Sho at 3000m and the Arima Kinen at 2500m. After this point, she won another 3 G1's at 2200m, 3000m and 3200m. While also coming 2nd at 2000m, 4th at 2200m, 5th at 3200m and 7th in her final Arima Kinen.  
  
Overall, an incredibly unstable career. Featuring some very high highs and very low lows. Generally, she seems to perform very poorly under 2000m, and does her best at 2500m and above.  
  
She will not be a real competitor for us at the Asahi Hai, given her poor performance at 1600m. She might show up anyway because she's weird like that, but while she'll place well I do not expect her to win.  
  
When it comes to racing styles, she is one of the only runners to have won a G1 using every single racing style, so the answer is "who the fuck knows". She's likely to have a weak early career because she can't turn her incredible raw talent into actual results on the track, also because she's incredibly moody and difficult to train.  
  
I expect her to be a viable threat for our Triple Crown _if_ she enters the Japan Derby and Kikuka Sho, I do not expect her to win the Satsuki Sho. She also may not rack up enough earnings to enter the Triple Crown before the Kikua Sho, as she did in real life.  
  
  
**Cherval Grand**  
Cherval Grand has a very medicore racing record, only claiming a single G1 and two G2's in her _very long_ five year racing career. [Racing Record](https://www.jbis.jp/horse/0001152403/record/).  
  
All of her wins and placing races were between 2000m and 3000m. But notably, in her graded races she only placed well in races from 2400m upwards. The shorter the race, the worse she performed. She should only be considered as a notable contender for anything at 2400m and above.  
  
I won't list her placing in races, since she ran 26 graded races, but out of those she placed in the top five 16 times.  
  
Her G1 win at the Japan Cup can be treated as a freak occurence more than anything. She is capable of placing in G1 races, but does not appear to have the ability to really break into first place.  
  
She is, theoretically, a viable contendor for the Kikuka Sho given her excellent performance at longer distances, and her 2nd place Tenno Sho Spring (3200m). But overall I just consider her to be a weaker racer than the rest of the competition. She also had a very poor early career, it took her about a year and a half to win her first G2 race. With this in mind, I do not believe she will earn entry into the Triple Crown. She _may_ attempt the Kikuka Sho, in which case she is a prime threat, but she may also not.  
  
She is zero threat to our attempts at the Asahi Hai, even if she could enter she would not win.  
  
Her racing style is primarily a pace chaser, but she has some ability as a late surger as well.  
  
  
**Durandal**  
Yet another case of a late bloomer. Durandal finished her career with a respectable 3 G1 victories, and 3 G1 second places. [Racing Record](https://www.jbis.jp/horse/0000619835/record/).  
  
Durandal performed well in un-graded stakes, taking four 1sts and one 2nd between 1200m and 1600m. However they absolutely bombed their attempt at the 1600m Mile Championship, coming in _10th_. After that they performed rather poorly, coming 9th in a 1800m G2 and 3rd in a 1200m G3. But after this point they had their late blooming, taking back to back G1's at 1200m and 1600m. Then back to back 2nd places at 1200m, before claiming their final G1 victory of their career at 1600m.  
  
Overall, Durandal is a _very_ strong Sprinter, with their optimal range stretching from 1200m to 1600m, but they seem to crash and burn the second they attempt to go any further, such as 1800m. And while they are a strong sprinter, they are also a late bloomer, as odd as that is to say. They performed well in un-graded races, but it took until their Seinor years to win a single graded race.  
  
I believe she will attempt to contest the Asahi Hai, but given her status as a late bloomer, I would not expect her to perform well. The un-graded 1600m race she won was competed in a time of 1:35.4, the G1 1600m that she came _10th_ in was completed at 1:33.3. Needless to say, there is an entire galaxy of difference in competitiveness between graded and un-graded races. The later G1 1600M she took 1st place in was also completed in 1:33.3, which doesn't speak of any improvement in her own racing ability, but merely that the competition was _much weaker_ than it was previously.  
  
She will not be a competitor for our Triple Crown challenge, not even slightly. If we do end up facing her in the Asahi Hai, she is an end closer (in sprints???), so there isn't much interaction with our racing style for her, nor should we bother.  
  
  
**Aston Machan**

Not much to comment on her career, of her first 5 races, one ungraded 1st, two G3 1st's and one G1 2nd. Later on she took a G1 1st on heavy track before plummeting to 14th and 10th in her next two races before she died.  
  
Performs very well between 1200m and 1600m. Was a strong competitor straight out of the gate. While she didn't aim for the Asahi Hai irl, it's just barely within her range, and she may compete for it. Zero chance of her being a threat in the Triple Crown.  
  
Mostly ran as a frontrunner and pace chaser, plenty of chances to mess with her with tactics and frenzy.  
  
  
**Vivlos**  
Vivlos dear, what are you doing in this generation??? A very competitive miler with 2 G1 wins and 3 G1 2nd places. [Racing Record](https://www.jbis.jp/horse/0001169235/record/).  
  
Her early career is a bit weird, as she performed well in her ungraded races winning one and coming 2nd in another, but absolutely bombed the two G3's she tried on the Triple Tiara route, coming in 12th back to back. After that she took her next G2 race 2nd and won the 2000m G1 right after. Very inconsistent early career. Afterwards she took 5th in a G2 at 1800m, 1st at the 1800m Dubai Turf G1, 2nd at 1800m G2 then 5th at the 2200m QE stakes. She later comes 2nd in 3 G1 races and 4th/8th in two others.  
  
Overall, her early career shows enormous trouble adapting to graded races, but once that is shaken off, she performs incredibly strongly between 1600m and 2000m. It took her an entire year from debut, Oct-Oct to win her first G1 on the Triple Tiara route, and after that she regularly placed well in her races, but did not win often. She regularly dipped down to 4th, 5th and 8th place in her races, which shows a strong inconsistency in strength. Notably, she ended her career with a 2nd in a G1, where 1st was taken by _Almond Eye_.  
  
She may be a strong concern for the Asahi Hai _if_ she enters. Her noted issues with graded races at the start of her career likely means she will not earn a place in the Asahi Hai. But if she does appear, she's a strong threat to take note of. Running primarily as a Late Surger with some ability as an End Closer.  
  
I do not believe she is a competitor for our triple crown route. She has not shown the distance aptitude, and her results drop sharply once she moves over 2000m. Additionally, she appears to be aiming for the Triple Tiara anyway.  
  
  
**Verxina**  
A potentially legendary career, cut short by a rival she could not surpass. Owning 2 G1 wins, _four_ G1 2nd places blocked by Gentildonna and one G1 3rd place. [Racing Record](https://www.jbis.jp/horse/0001107937/record/).  
  
Her career started strong and kept going strong. Winning a G3 race on the Tiara Route, only to be blocked in her next two G1 races at 1600m and 2400m by Gentildonna, taking 2nd in both. This continued in another G2 and G1 race at 1800m and 2000m, taking 2nd place to Gentildonna. Her next G1 was _another_ 2nd place at 2200m. After a poorly run G2 she then claimed her first G1 at 1600m. After this she has a string of 6 races, mixed G1 and G2, where she does not place above 7th. Before finally winning the second G1 of her career at 1600m. Her next G1 at 2200m comes in at 3rd before her last two G1's are 11th in the QE stakes and _14th_ at the Arima Kinen.  
  
From this we can see that she had an incredibly strong early career, but one that was blocked by an even greater monster in Gentildonna. The rest of her showcased a sharp drop in performance, with only a few bright spots where she rallied for singular races, but otherwise typically coming in from 7th-11th place.  
  
She has showcased very strong performance from 1600m to 2400m in her early career, but after her first year she only won 1600m races and placed worse and worse the longer the races got. But seeing as she is aiming for the Triple Tiara, it is unlikely that she will be present for either the Asahi Hai or the Triple Crown. Not a threat, but in the unlikely event she shows up for the Asahi Hai, a high priority to focus on.

The last win in a G2 at 2400m, before their final two G1 races at 2400m and 2500m, both coming 6th.

They do not have a particuarly strong aptitude for mile races, and while they may be pressured into attending the Asahi Hai if we are, I do not consider them a strong contendor.

**Kitasan Black**  
The big 7 G1 winner herself, who can also claim a G1 2nd place and 4 G1 3rd places. An absolute monster by any description, a terrifying 12 wins in 20 stats, if notably a late bloomer. [Racing Record](https://www.jbis.jp/horse/0001155349/).  
  
An easy sweep up to the Triple Crown, picking up two ungraded wins at 1800m and 2000m, with one G2 at 1800m. Coming in 3rd in the Satsuki Sho 2000m, then absolutely _bombing_ the Japan Derby in 14th before returning strong by winning a G2 at 2200m and then the Kikuka Sho at 3000m. A respectable 3rd in the Arima Kinen, 2nd in a G2 at 2000m then winning the G1 Tenno Sho Spring at 3200m. Once again 3rd in a G1 at 2200m, before winning a G2 and the G1 Japan Cup at 2400m. After which losing the Arima Kinen to Satono Diamond in 2nd, and winning two more G1's at 2000m and 3200m. Setting the (still standing) world record for 3200m turf in the process. After which there is a G1 9th at 2200m, 1st at 2000m in heavy track, 3rd at 2400m and then a 1st in the final Arima Kinen.  
  
Needless to say, Kitasan Black had a monstrous career, with the exception of two races where they performed incredibly poorly and were definitely flukes, they never fell below 3rd place at any distance they raced. While they notably performed much stronger at longer distances, they also performed weaker the shorter the distance. While they won several G1's below 2400m, this was also their weakest distance by far.  
  
Their shortest race was 1800m, and while they did win, they were definitely their least convincing victories. There is a slim possibility that they show up for the Asahi Hai, but if they do they are absolutely a top contendor, if less of a threat than others that specialise in Miles.  
  
She is going to be one of our most dangerous Triple Crown opponents for sure, right up there with Mihono Bourbon and Satono Diamond. While she bombed the Derby irl, that was absolutely a fluke of a performance and should be ignored.  
  
Ran almost every race as a front runner, but is a capable pace chaser.

She is a huge threat that we need to pay special attention to if she shows up in any of her races, because she's both strong and incredibly consistent. She maintained her strong performance for her entire career.  
  
She _shouldn't_ be showing up to the Asahi Hai as she's on the Triple Tiara route, but if she does we're in deep shit.  
  
She's extremely unlikely to appear in the Triple Crown route, as she's aiming for the Triple Tiara. Not a concern. (God bless the Tiara filies, that's going to be an absolute fucking bloodbath.)  
  
  
**Rice Shower**  
Rice has a very storied career, featuring 3 G1 victories, a G1 second place and a G1 third place. She notably swiped the Kikuka Sho from Mihono Bourbon, and the Tenno Sho from Mejiro McQueen. [Racing Record](https://www.jbis.jp/horse/0000224986/).  
  
Her career started off quite oddly, winning her debut at 1000m, bombing a G3 at 1200m, winning another ungraded race at 1600m then taking 4th in a G2 at 1800m. After which she took 8th at the Satsuki Sho 2000m, 8th at G2 2000m and then _second_ at the Japan Derby 2400m, right after Mihono Bourbon. After which she claimed two more G2's seconds at 2200m before winning the Kikuka Sho 3000m from Mihono Bourbon and setting a track record. She then bombed the Arima Kinen at 8th, before picking up a G2 second and then victory at 2500m. Next they took the Tenno Sho Spring 3200m from Mejiro McQueen, setting another track record before proceeding to take 6th, 14th and 8th in 3 G1's at 2000m, 2400m and 2500m. Her next Arima Kinen was a 3rd and she won the next G1 Tenno Sho Spring.  
  
Her track record is pretty wonky in consistency, usually in the top 3 for races over 2400m, but half the time falling to 6-14th. Overall, a very strong medium-long distance runner, but her execution leaves something to be desired as she cannot consistently show off her strength. Generally a pace chaser, but can run front.  
  
There _is_ a chance she shows up to the Asahi Hai, because her early racing record was weird with shorter races, but she probably isn't going to do too well there.  
  
Not a strong contendor for the Satsuki Sho, but she is an absolute terror for the Japan Derby and the Kikuka Sho. A favourite for the Kikuka Sho alongside Kitasan Black, really. A huge threat.
