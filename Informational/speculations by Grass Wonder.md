I was rereading the Q&A and this caught my attention. Not because of Special Week, but it made me think of Grass Wonder's character arc here.  
  
Grass is the weeb child of weeb parents. All of them love Japan. They love the land, they love the people, and they love the culture. Personally, I have not studied Japanese culture in depth. I read manga sometimes and have seen some anime. I can not compare my understanding of Japan to Grass Wonder's. She has actually studied it before she started living there.  
  
That said, the thing about being any kind of culture-lover, is that you're very in love with the idea of a culture, and usually it's popculture. And while Grass isn't that much of a tourist, her whole yamato-nadishiko thing is very much a product of her emulating the older idea of the ideal Japanese woman. She loves the idea of that culture at it's best.  
  
Now that she's stood up for Ruby in such a public fashion, she's going to start facing it at it's worst.  
  
Being fair, it's not just Japanese culture she'll be dealing with, but more uma racing culture. But this is a japanese franchise taking place in Japan, so that's not much of a meaningful difference. Apparently being a G1 winner on a celebrated team isn't enough of a social shield, according to her trainer.  
  
I don't think it's a question of if Grass can weather the public response, nor how other students will treat her. She has an iron will and knows she took the moral high ground. She encouraged a girl who was never allowed to race to win. That much is uncomplicated.  
  
But how will this effect her view of japanese culture, now that some might see her as just as much of a valid target as Ruby? Only a fool would dare try to bully Grass Wonder, but the world has no shortage of fools. Some might decide that an American acting all japanese is now offensive. Some xenophobes might just start taking easy pot shots. Some might say that associating with the cheater's daughter calls all her wins into question.  
  
Again, I can't see this getting her to stop supporting Ruby. If anything it'd make her dig in more. But many people attacking her character, being haters online. Would that harm her love for Japan? After all, it's easy to love something that seems to love you back. When that something turns on you, that's a very different matter.  
  
Might make her respect Ruby more though. Getting a real taste of what Ruby has to live with, and she still chooses to be a kind, hardworking, and dedicated student? Ruby is a rare type of individual, and this would let grass appreciate that on a level that comes only with experience.
