Skill values were calculated at 10 + (ATTRIBUTE VALUE x SCALING). Tagged Skills were then given a +15 bonus. You can increase your skills by levelling up, completing objectives and raising Social Links.  
  
You may assume the following about Skills and Attributes:  

- 0 is nothing, 20-25 is beginner, 40-50 is average, 80-100 is good, and 130-150 is great. Checks will use multiples of 5.
- Tagged skills automatically gain +5 whenever you level up.
- More skills may be unlocked in the future. The list is not exhaustive.
- There will be checks exclusive to Attributes or Skills (similar to the Fallout games). Not all checks are related to racing. Racing skills may be applicable outside racing.
- Reassigning a skill's scaling is possible.

Traits are special Perks representative of your character's history.

**Orphaned**  
Let's not mince words. Your mother abandoned you as a child. Didn't even bother to leave a note, or give you a kiss on the forehead before she up and vanished, leaving you behind in the Satono family mansion all by yourself. You escaped soon after. You spent a great deal of your childhood drifting from street to street, foster home to foster home. As a result, you grew some unconventional smarts, as well as a tendency to favor those your own age…  

- Social Links with academy students and other underaged individuals are easier.
- Social Links with Trainers and other adult individuals are harder.
- +15 to all Social skills and +1 to Wits.

  
**Micro Cosmos**  
Dreaming dead, waking life. Stress and hardship built up to a breaking point, whereupon a piece of your mind detached from its fleshy prison and began orbiting around your soul. Vividness stains your dreams. There are now shadows that lengthen under the sun; and thrums and pulses neither human nor umamusume around you seem to hear. On the plus side, your artistic capabilities are flourishing.  

- More mysterious, occult and/or paranoid choices and encounters will occur.
- More dreams (and nightmares) will occur.
- Grants you the World Resonance skill (Esoteric, scales with INS). You will communicate with things that should not be communicated with...
- +20 to Artistry.

  
**Underground Umadol**  
Why do umamusume sing and dance? Because they can, alright? You're telling me if you had the opportunity to dress in gorgeous outfits and sing your heart out to attract the attention of thousands, you wouldn't take it, you utter numbs—Ahem. Anyway, you decided to join the underground idol industry to get a headstart on the whole theatric side of the umamusume racing gig. Your past slowed your progress, of course, but you found out plenty of idol fans are willing to overlook past sins if their oshi has a pretty face and a cute pout.  

- Gain the Social Media skill (Social, scales with PSY). This lets you perform umafluencer feats, such as posing for sick photos and ratioing someone on Umatter. You can gain additional Prestige this way too.
- +15 to Dancing and Foreign Languages. But idols have to look cute and adorable, so -15 to Intimidate.
- You still have some old contacts, including fans who still worship you as their oshi. That's worth something...right?
