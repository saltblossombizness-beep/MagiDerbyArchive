**OTHER STATS**  
  
**Prestige**.  
Represents how famous you are. You gain it from placing well in races, winning awards, progressing Social Links and anything else that gets your name out there. The more Prestige you accumulate, the more doors and opportunities open for you.  
  
**Alignment**.  
A measure of how your character stands. There are two axes: Lawful or Chaotic & Light or Dark.  
  
Lawful characters abide by the order and authority, whereas Chaotic characters disregard said order for personal freedom and autonomy. They are wholly defined by your own actions.  
  
Light and Dark, however, are representative of society's perspective of you. Light characters are treated as the heroes, whereas Dark characters are seen as villains, similar to the face and heel terms from wrestling, respectively. A favorite such as Tokai Teio, who is seen as the hero of the turf, would receive the Light alignment. Rice Shower, despite being a sweet and gentle girl in reality, would receive the Dark alignment for destroying multiple winstreaks and crushing the hopes of fans.  
  
Note that Light and Dark are not equal to good and evil. The only measure of good and evil that matters is your very own.

**Racing Affinities**  
Umamusume run in four styles:  

- Front Runner: Starts and ends the race trying to be the leader of the pack. Speed is king. Power is also used for final spurts.
- Pace Chaser: Stays towards the front of the pack, trying to keep up with the leader, requiring Stamina to do so and then Power to overtake.
- Late Surger: Starts at the back of the pack and then accelerates to the front at the final straight. Needs a lot of Power but can conserve somewhat on Stamina. Guts is also used to ensure the final burst of acceleration.
- End Closer: Starts at last place and then overtakes everyone at the end when they're tiring. Needs Speed, Stamina and Power.

  
They also run on two main types of ground: Turf or Dirt.  
  
Affinities are measured with a binary Learned/Unlearned system. Checks will become easier or harder depending on what affinities you possess or lack.

**MAIN MECHANICS**  
  
**EXP**  
Pass stat checks to gain EXP. Enough EXP and you gain a LEVEL UP.  

- If a check passes by 0-10 (for skills) or 0-1 (for Attributes), then you gain 10 EXP instead of 5. This is the accelerated EXP gain granted by the Fallen Heiress difficulty.
- Skill checks with difficulty equal to **less than half** of the attempted value will count as trivial and not grant EXP.
- Attribute checks with difficulty equal to -3 or lower than the attempted value will count as trivial and not grant EXP.

  
**Levelling up**  
Things that always happen during a single LEVEL UP:  

- Tagged skills get their +5 bonus.
- Increase Mood by 1 stage if Mood is negative. Decrease Mood by 1 stage if Mood is positive. Nothing happens if Mood is neutral.
- EXP requirement threshold increases by 20.
- +15 skill points to spend without restriction.
- +3 AP to spend in the shop.

Things that happen sometimes during a LEVEL UP:  

- New Perk is unlocked every third level. (These almost always have positive effects.)
- +1 to one tagged Attribute every third level. (This will be voted on)

Notes:  

- All options in the AP shop may be chosen multiple times unless stated otherwise.
- Any AP not spent will be discarded.
- Increasing skills and Attributes is possible outside of LEVEL UPs. (It's already happened before)
- Increasing AP earned per LEVEL UP is possible.
- Multiple LEVEL UPs will be conducted in the same votes. We'll be using normal Plan voting with at least a **12 hour** moratorium. This is to ensure voters across multiple time zones have the chance to contribute.
- The order in which options are applied is: Attributes are modified, then any changed scaling is applied, then skills are modified, and finally anything else happens.

All LEVEL UPs will occur at the end of Encounters.  
  
**Encounters**  
Encounters are major story segments within the quest. They will use the following progression loop:  

- Training Time. (See Team Stats threadmark)
- Daily Life Planning. You'll spend TP (Time Points) on specific Events to decide what Ruby does. (More about this will be revealed later.)
- QM rolls for a few random Events (less than 4).
- Daily Life Beginnings. Voting for Event choices happens here.
- Daily Life Resolution.
- Plot stuff happens. (May be optional; will almost certainly take longer than Daily Life.)
- Repeat until End of Encounter.
- End of Encounter. Any LEVEL UPs are processed here.

There is no strict in-universe time limit for any of these items.  
  
The exception to this loop will occur when a race happens. Points 2-5 will be replaced by the buildup, climax and aftermath of the Race itself. No TP will be spent, and no voting will occur except for matters pertaining to the race itself, and all random events will be replaced with random mini-events. Training Time and plot stuff will remain consistent.

**NIGHTMARE EXPLORATION**  
  
The Nightmare does not tolerate breaches into its system. It will attempt to destroy you by sending all manner of entities at you, ranging from monsters, and artifacts to anomalous locations and traps. To overcome this, you must perform Nightmare Exploration, in which you receive the entities the Nightmare commands, and deal with them while attempting to derive as much reward and bear as little physical & mental damage as possible.  
  
**Main Rules**  
At the beginning of Exploration, the QM rolls 2d12 for X amount of events, where is X a number determined by difficulty and plot context (minimum: three). Unlike Daily Life events, Nightmare Exploration uses a modified table with different weights and the result capping out at Good. Critical failure and critical hits are preserved as normal.  
  
The vast majority of exploration events will present an entity for you to deal with. The narration will analyze the entity, make a judgment in accordance with any skill checks & its Observation Rate (i.e. how much knowledge you possess about the entity's nature, behavior, history, etc.), and present 1+ choices. Choices will either result in a positive, neutral or negative outcome, depending on how the entity functions.  
Not all entities are necessarily hostile to you, but a great deal of them will be.  
  
An Exploration event's difficulty is based both on the dice results and the overall Exploration Difficulty, which will be presented at the before the events are rolled.  

- Events are singleton and do not affect each other.
- There will always be an option to engage.
- There will always be an option to attempt to disengage.
- The higher the Observation Rate, the more knowledge about the entity is displayed, the easier interacting with it becomes, and the further along its story shall progress.
- Observation Rate cannot decrease. Some choices, however, may increase it faster than others (minimum increase: 15%).
- Some choices offer active Attribute checks. _(ATTRIBUTE_LEVEL + a 2d12 die roll)_ must equal or surpass a numerical threshold, which will be listed. The intensity of penalties for failure will be listed as well. Passing these checks will generally offer positive results. These checks' difficulties may increase or decrease, dependent on skill checks.
- These checks are unaffected by starting gate Advantage/Disadvantage dice, unless they involve starting gates.
- Future teammates can perform for skill or Attribute checks in your stead.

Very rarely will an Exploration event not contain any entities to deal with.  
  
**Global Observation Rate**  
The QM is well aware that RNG may be frustrating. To compensate, a Global Observation Rate will be offered. It will increase at a slower rate (minimum: 8.5%), but will do so regardless of an Event's outcome. Once it reaches 100%, it will provide high amounts of information for the most unknown entity in the lineup, then reset back down to 0%.  
  
**Health and Morale**  
Your Health and Morale are at risk during Exploration. Positive outcomes may restore either; negative outcomes may reduce either.  

- Health is now calculated through _1 + (Highest Physical Attribute / 2)._
- Morale is now calculated through _1 + (Highest Mental Attribute / 2)._
- Health and Morale will be gained or lost in values of 1 and more.
- Health and Morale levels will persist past Exploration and into any content that occurs within the Nightmare.

**Exploration checks and rewards**  
Checks within Exploration events do not grant EXP. Engaging with an event will always grant 5 EXP, with further rewards determined based on choice outcomes. (e.g. emerging unscathed, maxing out an entity's Observation Rate, etc.).  
  
Further rewards can include:  

- More EXP
- Temporary stat buffs
- Permanent stat increases
- Advantage starting gate dice
- Further Observation Rate increases
- Special items
- Information

  
Penalties for failing can include:  

- Lost EXP (if Godolphin's Grace from your regalia is somehow disabled)
- Temporary stat debuffs
- Permanent stat debuffs (if Godolphin's Grace from your regalia is somehow disabled)
- Disadvantage starting gate dice
- Loss of information
